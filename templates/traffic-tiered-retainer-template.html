2. Retainer & Scope of Services

The Client specifically retains {PARALEGAL_NAME}, Licensed Paralegal (LSO #{PARALEGAL_LSO}), to provide legal services in connection with a charge under the Provincial Offences Act, the Highway Traffic Act, the Compulsory Automobile Insurance Act, or related provincial offence legislation. The Paralegal acts only within the scope of practice authorized by By-Law 4 of the Law Society of Ontario for Class P1 paralegals. Nothing in this Agreement creates a solicitor-client relationship with a lawyer or an obligation to provide services outside paralegal scope.

Included in this retainer

  • Review of the Offence Notice and prosecution disclosure
  • Filing the Notice of Intention to Appear within 15 days
  • Disclosure request and review
  • First appearance and procedural attendances
  • Early Resolution Meeting or prosecutor pre-trial
  • Plea negotiations on Client instructions
  • Trial preparation and witness preparation
  • One (1) trial date at the Ontario Court of Justice
  • Section 11 reopening application on Form 102, where available
  • Routine client communication throughout the matter

Not included (separate retainer)

  • Appeals under Part VII or to the Court of Appeal (s. 139)
  • Criminal Code driving charges
  • MTO suspension review or driver-improvement interviews
  • Insurance disputes, rate or coverage appeals
  • Civil claims arising from the incident
  • Judicial review
  • Additional trial dates beyond the first
  • Client-caused adjournments
  • Reopening motions beyond the initial Form 102
  • CVOR, immigration, and employment consequences

Collateral consequences. This retainer does not cover MTO administrative hearings, roadside Administrative Driver’s Licence Suspensions under HTA s. 48.3 or s. 172 (which run by operation of law), insurance underwriting decisions, CVOR consequences for commercial drivers, immigration consequences, employment consequences, or post-disposition enforcement issues.

4. Outcomes, Plea Authority & Settlement

No Guaranteed Outcome

The Client acknowledges that no result has been promised or guaranteed. Outcomes depend on the law, the evidence, credibility findings, and the decision of the Justice of the Peace or Judge. The Paralegal does not control the prosecutor, the Court, the Ministry of Transportation, or the Client’s insurer.

Demerit Points & Insurance — No Prediction

The Paralegal cannot predict or guarantee how any insurer will assess the charge, conviction, amendment, withdrawal, plea, or final disposition for underwriting purposes. Insurance consequences depend on the insurer’s own rules, the Client’s driving history, and other factors outside the Paralegal’s control. Clients with insurance concerns should seek written confirmation from their insurer or broker.

Plea Authority & Client Control

The Paralegal will conduct plea negotiations in accordance with the Client’s instructions. The decision whether to accept any plea offer remains with the Client. The Paralegal will not enter a plea or accept a resolution without the Client’s express authorization. Paying the set fine on the back of the Offence Notice (Option 1) is a plea of guilty under the Provincial Offences Act and disposes of the charge; the Client agrees not to take that step without consulting the Paralegal.

Settlement

Resolution may involve compromise, including a plea to a lesser offence. The Paralegal will provide advice, but the final decision rests with the Client unless the Client has given express written settlement authority.

6. Hearings, Accommodation & Funding Requirements

Hearing Attendance & Virtual Proceedings

The Paralegal will attend on the Client’s behalf where the Provincial Offences Act permits a paralegal to appear without the defendant. The Client must be available by phone during any appearance and must attend in person where the Court requires personal attendance. Failure to attend, where required, may result in conviction in absentia or other adverse consequences. Hearings may occur virtually by Zoom or audio conference as directed by the Court; the Client agrees to ensure a quiet location, working device, and stable internet for any virtual appearance.

Accommodation, Interpretation & Client Understanding

The Client shall promptly advise the Paralegal of any disability-related accommodation needs affecting communication, attendance, or participation, so Court accommodations can be requested. If interpretation or translation services are required, the Client remains responsible for the associated costs unless otherwise agreed in writing. The Client remains responsible for ensuring that they fully understand the proceedings and any communications; if anything is unclear, the Client agrees to say so and request clarification.

Urgent or Last-Minute Matters

If the Client retains the Paralegal close to a trial date or after a deadline has nearly expired, preparation time may be limited and strategy may be restricted by available time, evidence, and Court scheduling. The Paralegal is not responsible for prejudice caused by late retention or delayed disclosure.

No Action Without Funding

The Paralegal is not obliged to commence, continue, or complete any step (including filing the Notice of Intention to Appear, attending Court, or attending trial) unless the Client’s account is current and any required deposit has been received in the firm’s trust account. Delay caused by non-payment is at the Client’s risk, including missing the 15-day deadline and being convicted in absentia under s. 9.1 of the Provincial Offences Act.

7. Recording, Witnesses, Media & Online Conduct

Recording, Witnesses & Media

Ontario is a one-party-consent jurisdiction. Without limiting any lawful right to record, the Client agrees: not to publish, disseminate, or misuse recordings of communications with the Paralegal or firm personnel; that recordings of privileged communications may impair or waive privilege and is discouraged from recording privileged calls without notice; not to contact the prosecutor, the issuing officer, the Court, or potential witnesses directly without the Paralegal’s knowledge; not to send communications that undermine legal strategy; and not to communicate with media or journalists about the proceeding without first consulting the Paralegal.

Online & Social-Media Conduct — Do Not Post, Do Not Delete

The Client agrees not to post, comment, or share anything about the charge, the underlying incident, the officer, or the proceeding on any social-media or online platform. Posts can be subpoenaed, can damage credibility, and can be cited against the Client at trial.

The Client is also instructed not to delete existing online content that may relate to the proceeding without first consulting the Paralegal. Deletion can itself amount to spoliation of evidence.
