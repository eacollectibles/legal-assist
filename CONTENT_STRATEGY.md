## 2. Target audience (inferred)

**Primary buyer personas:**

1. **Distressed tenant** — has received a landlord notice (N4/N5/N12/N13), is being harassed, or unit is unlivable. Urgency: very high. Budget: low-to-moderate. Search behaviour: panic-driven, mobile, late-night.
2. **Small claims plaintiff/defendant** — service contractor dispute, unpaid invoice, vehicle damage, security deposit recovery. Budget: moderate. Compares paralegal vs lawyer.
3. **Ticket recipient** — speeding, careless driving, stunt driving, photo radar. Urgency: 15-day filing window. Budget: low. Insurance-cost-driven decision.
4. **HRTO claimant** — workplace discrimination, housing discrimination, disability accommodation refused. Urgency: 1-year limitation. Budget: low (often unemployed/displaced).
5. **Dismissed employee** — released, terminated, constructive dismissal. Reading severance offer in hand. Budget: moderate (often using last paycheque).
6. **WSIB claimant** — denied benefits, return-to-work dispute, lost wages. Budget: low.

## 6. Internal Linking Plan

Every new article must link to:

1. **The relevant pillar service page** (e.g., `/landlord-tenant-board`, `/small-claims-court`, `/hrto`)
2. **The closest "next-step" CTA page** (e.g., contact form, retainer page, "how it works")
3. **2–3 sibling blog posts** in the same cluster (lateral reinforcement)
4. **1 parent-cluster anchor link** (e.g., a "Tenant Applications" hub if you build one)

**Suggested new hub pages to build alongside the blog:**

- `/landlord-tenant-board/tenant-applications` — hub linking all T-application articles
- `/landlord-tenant-board/eviction-notices` — hub linking all N-notice articles
- `/small-claims-court/enforcement` — hub linking collection, garnishment, examination articles
- `/hrto/discrimination-grounds` — hub linking each ground article
- `/provincial-offences/insurance-and-licence` — hub linking demerit, suspension, insurance impact articles

These hubs are short pages (300–500 words) that exist purely to bind clusters together — they earn rankings AND distribute link equity to the deep articles.

**Anchor text rules:**
- Use descriptive, keyword-rich anchors ("how to set aside a default judgment in Ontario") not generic ("click here")
- Link the *first* mention of a topic the first time it appears in an article
- Cap each article at ~6 internal links to avoid dilution

## 7. Content Types to Layer In

You're 100 % blog-post heavy right now. Diversify with:

- **Decision-tree articles** — "Do I have a case?" interactive flows. High engagement, high backlink potential.
- **Calculators** — "Estimate your severance" (Bardal-factor based), "Estimate your retainer cost", "Estimate your eviction timeline". You already have `TicketQuoteCalculator` — extend the pattern. Calculators routinely earn 10×–100× more backlinks than blog posts.
- **Glossary / dictionary pages** — "Ontario Legal Terms" indexed for snippet capture
- **Comparison pages** — "Paralegal vs Lawyer", "HRTO vs Small Claims for Workplace Harassment", "ESA Claim vs Wrongful Dismissal Suit"
- **Case-result snippets** (anonymised) — "Reduced N12 case to $0 + month rent compensation" — these build trust AND attract long-tail queries
- **Form walkthroughs** — Form 9A (Defence), Form 11A (Counterclaim), T6, L1, etc. with annotated screenshots
- **Original data** — annual "Ontario tenant rights survey" or "Average severance offered in Ontario by industry". Original research earns press coverage and backlinks.
